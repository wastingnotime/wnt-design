# WNT-160 logo selection

Issue: [WNT-160](https://linear.app/wastingnotime/issue/WNT-160).
Prepared 2026-10-15. Status: **B — Forward W selected; kit v1.0.0 produced and locally validated**.

## First exploration

The [comparison board](../assets/explorations/wnt-160/concepts-01.png) shows three
directions in a channel avatar, Community Lab header and secondary Sliding Tasks
maker signature. It was generated with the built-in image generation tool using
the [recorded prompt](../assets/explorations/wnt-160/prompt-01.txt).
This is a raster exploration, not approved artwork or an export master.

| Direction | Intent | Refinement to evaluate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — Time into motion | Simplified hourglass with a forward arrow in negative space | Keep the arrow opening clear at favicon sizes and avoid a fragile central join |
| B — Forward W | Folded W with an ascending terminal | Remove dependence on hairline fold gaps; assess whether the silhouette is distinctive enough |
| C — Maker monogram | Integrated geometric W/N | Improve immediate letter recognition and preserve the diagonal gap at small sizes |

Initial recommendation: A, because it retains the established time association
while reducing the existing outlined hourglass's internal detail. This is a
recommendation, not a selection. B and C remain alternatives for a more abstract
maker identity.

The board includes generated shading and approximate type/color rendering. Its
repeated marks are visual approximations rather than identical vector instances.
Final masters must use deterministic geometry and flat colors. The footer samples
illustrate hierarchy; they do not prescribe Sliding Tasks typography or layout.
No actual 16/32px, contrast, keyboard or crop validation has been completed here.

## Selection and delivery

Henrique selected **B — Forward W** on 2026-10-15: “i prefer B, is very
distinguishable.” This supersedes the initial recommendation of A. The selected
identity is the folded W with an ascending right terminal. Production geometry
keeps four panels, removes generated shading, and opens the fold separations.

Delivered: [identity v1.0.0](../assets/identity/v1.0.0/README.md),
[usage rules](identity/README.md), [specimen](identity/v1.0.0-specimen.png),
[validation evidence](identity/validation-v1.0.0.md), and a reproducible exporter.
Consumer rollout remains in WNT-161–165; final deployed verification is WNT-166.

### Original delivery checklist

The following requirements guided the kit delivery:

1. Construct and inspect editable SVG masters and wordmark lockups, with shared
   geometry for green-on-dark, dark-on-light and monochrome variants.
2. Validate actual 16/32px icons, small avatars, circle/square crops and restrained
   footer usage. Iterate the geometry if detail collapses at small sizes.
3. Define clear space, minimum sizes, background/contrast rules and crop-safe use.
4. Define linked EN/PT-BR maker signatures pointing to
   <https://wastingnotime.org/>, including accessible names and visible focus.
   Product identity stays primary; monochrome endorsement is supported.
5. Publish versioned favicon/avatar/social exports with provenance and a manifest,
   plus consumer guidance and YouTube banner/thumbnail signature rules.

The selected versioned kit is the consumer source. Existing imported assets and
the exploration board remain historical references, not production exports.
